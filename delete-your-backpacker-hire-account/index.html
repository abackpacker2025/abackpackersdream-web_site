Account and data deletion

Delete your Backpacker Hire account

You can request deletion of your Backpacker Hire account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the email address registered to your Backpacker Hire account.
  2. Use the subject Backpacker Hire account deletion request.
  3. Include the name of your business or account so we can identify it.

We may ask you to confirm account ownership before completing the request.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After verification, we will delete your Backpacker Hire account and personal information associated with the account, including your profile and business listing information. Content or records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or regulatory reasons may be kept only for the required period and then deleted or anonymised.

Need help?

Email admin@abackpackersdream.com.au. You can also review our privacy policy. Travelling, not hiring? Delete a Backpacker account instead.
