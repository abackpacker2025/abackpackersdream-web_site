Credits

A Backpacker’s Dream and Backpacker Hire are built on other people’s work. Thank you.

OpenStreetMap

Some listing and map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).

Many stays and hangouts in the apps started from OpenStreetMap data: a place’s name, type, address, position, and where available its phone number, website and opening hours. Each of those listings shows this credit. A business can claim its listing and change it in Backpacker Hire. To correct or remove a listing, email admin@abackpackersdream.com.au.

Maps in the apps

Maps in the iPhone apps are drawn by Apple Maps, and in the Android apps by Google Maps. Their own credits are shown on each map.

Fonts

This website uses Oswald, Inter and Instrument Serif from Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font License. The apps use Bricolage Grotesque and Hanken Grotesk, under the same licence.
