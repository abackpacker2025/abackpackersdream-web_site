How the 88 days work

“Specified work” is the work Home Affairs needs you to have done to get your next Working Holiday or Work and Holiday visa.

88 days

For a 2nd visa: 3 months of specified work on your first visa, which Home Affairs counts as at least 88 calendar days.

179 days

For a 3rd visa: 6 months of specified work on your second visa (done on or after 1 July 2019).

Full time

You need the same number of days or shifts a full-time employee in that job would work in that time. It can be split across employers.

Paid right

It must be paid at the correct legal wage. Keep your payslips. Only volunteer bushfire or disaster recovery work counts unpaid.

Good to know

Does hospitality count for the 88 days?

Only in Northern Australia and in Remote and Very Remote Australia, for both visas. Café, bar or hotel work in a regional city like Newcastle, Tamworth or Toowoomba doesn’t count. Airlie Beach, Cairns and Darwin do.

Do UK passport holders need to do 88 days?

No. Home Affairs says UK passport holders who apply on or after 1 July 2024 don’t need specified work for a second or third Working Holiday (417) visa.

Does a day of rain count?

Unpaid days off don't count, and that includes days lost to bad weather. Paid public holidays and paid sick days can count. Start early so a wet season doesn’t leave you short.

Can I work two jobs on the same day?

A calendar day only counts once, even if you work two shifts or for two employers that day.

How does the app know if a job may count?

The business picks the type of work when it posts the job, and we check that against the job’s postcode and the Home Affairs lists. From the next app update, jobs that may count show “✓ May count for 88 days”, and you can filter the Jobs tab to show only them.
