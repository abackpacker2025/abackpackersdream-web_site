I'm hiring

Run your hiring from your pocket.

Backpacker Hire is free to download. Plans, if you want one, are in-app purchases or bought through Stripe on this website. Post a job, see who's keen, and add photos to stand out. At launch, many job and stay listings are compiled from public information, starting with the Hunter Valley, and the business may not have reviewed them. An unclaimed listing shows “Not yet rated” and has no star rating. Stars appear only after the business has claimed the listing and joined, and they are the average of ratings users leave after that. To claim, correct or remove a listing, email admin@abackpackersdream.com.au. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
