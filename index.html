About

Built in the Hunter Valley,
for the regions.

A Backpacker’s Dream is two apps from one team: Backpacker, for travellers finding work, a bed, and people along their working holiday; and Backpacker Hire, for the local businesses who need them. We started in the Hunter Valley, and we’re building this the way we’d want it to work — for a backpacker turning up with a van and a visa, and for the farm, hostel, or pub trying to find good people fast. FAQ

Good to know.

Is Backpacker free to use?

Yes — it’s free to download and use to find jobs, places to stay, and hangouts.

Do I book accommodation through the app?

No. Accommodation listings are for discovery — you see what’s nearby, then contact and book directly with the stay.

Who puts the listings up?

Businesses post their own listings in Backpacker Hire. Some listings come from public information until the business claims them, starting with the Hunter Valley. A business may not have reviewed or endorsed such a listing, and the details may be out of date, so confirm them directly. Some listing and map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. See Credits. We are not affiliated with a listed business unless the listing says so. An unclaimed listing shows “Not yet rated” and has no star rating. Stars appear only after the business has claimed the listing and joined, and they are the average of ratings users leave after that. To claim, correct or remove a listing, email admin@abackpackersdream.com.au. We aim to reply within 5 business days. A business can also sign up in Backpacker Hire, which is free to download, and post its own listing.

Where does the app work?

We’re going national. Jobs are live in Newcastle and the Hunter. Stays and pubs are also live in Sydney, Melbourne, Byron Bay, the Gold Coast, Noosa, Airlie Beach and Cairns, and the rest of Australia opens area by area. Check the app for what’s live near you.

I run a business — is it free to list?

Backpacker Hire is free to download. Plans, if you want one, are in-app purchases or bought through Stripe on the website. A promo code works only on the channel and conditions stated with it.
